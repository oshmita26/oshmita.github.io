This post shows how to add bibliography to simple blog posts. We support every citation style that jekyll-scholar does. That means simple citation like (Sarkar & Sinha, 2023), multiple citations like (Sarkar & Sinha, 2023; Sarkar et al., 2024), long references like Sarkar, O., Parida, A., & Sinha, S. (2024). Automatic Number Plate Character Recognition using Paddle-OCR. https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10616305 or also quotes:
